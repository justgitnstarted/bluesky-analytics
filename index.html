Workforce Findings & Action Plan

Understand what is happening. Identify what needs attention. Decide what to do next.

Our reporting organizes what employees told us into a clear picture of the conditions affecting their experience. Depending on the engagement, findings include recurring themes, the context behind them, contributing conditions and open questions, prioritized recommendations, suggested owners and steps, and measures for tracking progress.

We distinguish what employees reported, what the analysis supports, and what needs further examination. Scope, deliverables, and follow-up support are defined before work begins.

Retained by employers. Clear about it.

Blue Sky Analytics is retained by employers, and we say so to every employee we speak with. We explain rights and representation accurately, address disputed claims with facts and sources, and leave the decision about representation where the law places it, with employees.

Who this is built for.

This is built for you if

  • You lead a multi-site or large-facility operation with an hourly workforce
  • A petition is pending, or organizing activity is nearby
  • You suspect something has shifted and want the data
  • You want the cause addressed, not only the election won

This is not for you if

How an engagement runs.

  1. Private conversation

    You describe the situation. We tell you whether and how we can help, and what it would involve.

  2. Scope

    Methods, information handling, deliverables, counsel coordination, and timeline are set in writing before work starts.

  3. Field work

    Campaign education on the floor, or a SentimentIQ deployment within five business days with a fourteen-day completion window.

  4. Findings

    A leadership debrief covering what employees reported, what the analysis supports, and what needs further examination.

  5. Action plan

    Prioritized recommendations with owners, steps, and the measures for tracking progress.

Questions we hear on the first call.

What makes Blue Sky's perspective useful?

Our consulting network includes former union officials and organizers with firsthand experience in employee representation and labor relations. We match consultants to the workforce by background and language, and we combine that perspective with workforce analytics and operational analysis.

Who retains Blue Sky?

Employers. We are clear about that with every employee we speak with, and we respect employees' right to make their own decision about representation.

Do you work with our existing labor counsel?

Yes. Most engagements run under the direction of the client's labor counsel. Responsibilities, methods, and scope are set before work begins. If you do not have dedicated labor counsel, we can bring affiliated attorneys who practice labor relations daily.

How does proactive assessment differ from campaign support?

Proactive assessment measures workplace conditions through SentimentIQ and interviews before any organizing activity. Campaign support is employee education during an active campaign, under a separate engagement process. We do not deploy surveys once a campaign has begun.

What does leadership receive?

The deliverables agreed for the engagement: findings, context, and recommendations. Reporting focuses on workplace themes. No group smaller than eight is reported, and clients never receive raw responses.

What happens after the findings?

We walk leadership through the findings and the recommended next steps. Management training, implementation support, or re-assessment are defined in the engagement scope.

How quickly will someone respond?

The same business day, through the contact channel you provide. Call or text (650) 519-6464 if you would rather not wait.
