Built for the floor, not the inbox.

Hourly workforces do not live in email. SentimentIQ meets employees where they are: a random access code, a phone or a computer, no app, no login, about ten minutes, on any shift.

Deployment is configured to your headcount, departments, and shift structure and is ready within five business days. Employees have a fourteen-day window to respond. Leadership watches participation in real time without any path to individual answers.

Seven dimensions. One picture of the workplace.

Every workforce condition we have seen precede organizing interest traces back to one or more of these dimensions. Measuring all seven at once, by department and shift, shows where the gaps are and how deep they run.

Management Effectiveness

Trust, fairness, and consistency of direct supervisors and the leadership chain.

Communication Quality

Whether employees feel informed, included, and respected in how decisions are explained.

Pay & Benefits

Perceived fairness of compensation relative to contribution and market.

Safety & Work Conditions

Physical environment, workload, scheduling equity, and whether employees believe the company looks after their wellbeing.

General Work Experience

Belonging, pride, morale, and whether the work feels meaningful day to day.

HR Effectiveness

Whether HR is seen as responsive, professional, consistent, and clear: concerns get a reply and an outcome, benefits are explained, policy is applied the same way for everyone, and confidences are kept.

Voice & Advocacy

Whether employees believe someone inside the company represents their interests. This is the dimension most closely tied to whether employees look outside the company for representation. It is measured through indirect indicators across the instrument, at the workforce level, and never for any individual.

Anonymity is the architecture, not a promise.

Employees give candid answers when the system itself keeps answers apart from names. SentimentIQ is built that way.

  • Employees respond through a random access code. No email, login, or app is required.
  • Participation is logged separately from answers.
  • No group smaller than eight is ever reported.
  • Clients receive aggregated findings and never raw responses.
  • The instrument has been reviewed by labor counsel.

There is no path from a report back to a person. The full protocol, including retention and storage, is on the Data Security page.

What leadership receives.

Scores are the starting point. The deliverable is a leadership team that knows where the gaps are, what is causing them, and what to do first.

Seven-dimension findings report

Scored results across all seven dimensions, with department and shift breakdowns above the respondent floor, and cross-location comparison where applicable.

Department and shift risk ranking

Where the low scores concentrate, so leadership knows which teams and which conditions to look at first.

Root cause analysis

Structured interviews with management and a cross-section of employees, read against the scores, to identify the operating decisions and practices behind each gap.

Workforce Findings & Action Plan

Prioritized recommendations with suggested owners, implementation steps, and the measures for tracking progress, delivered in a leadership debrief.
